## Pattern in the SERPs

1. Almost every result is either a **directory** or a **single-service brand page**.
2. Problem queries ("AC water leakage repair near me", "refrigerator door not closing") have
   dedicated JustDial pages but few genuine local business pages answering them.
3. "Near me" and "emergency" language is common, but most pages do not show a real local
   street address with matching schema.
4. No competitor result combines: multi-appliance coverage + real Kottayam address + two
   verified phone routes + 50 visible first-party review cards + clear visit charge.
