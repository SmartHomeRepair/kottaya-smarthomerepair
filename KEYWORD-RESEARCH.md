## Live SERP observations (why these keywords matter)

Searches run on 2026-11-22 for Kottayam home-appliance repair showed:

- JustDial has a category page "Home Appliance Repair Services in Kottayam" listing **106 providers**
- Sulekha has locality-specific pages, e.g. "Home Appliances Repair Service in Changanassery, Kottayam" and "Ponkunnam, Kottayam"
- Dedicated competitor landing pages exist for single services, e.g. "AC Service Kottayam", "Voltas AC Repair Service Kottayam", "Daikin AC Service Kottayam", "LG Repair Service in Kottayam"
- Problem-based queries are actively targeted: "AC water leakage repair near me", "refrigerator door not closing", "AC power on issues"
- "Near me" and "emergency" variants appear in Kottayam-specific pages and commentary
- The parent brand already ranks in Kochi with `smarthomerepair.in` service pages, which gives this subdomain a strong entity/topical base

**Conclusion:** the winnable space is not a broad "repair" term. It is service + city
("AC repair Kottayam") and problem + city ("AC not cooling Kottayam"), backed by a real local
address, phone number, hours and 50 visible customer reviews.
